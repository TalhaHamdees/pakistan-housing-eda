## Hedonic regression (area fixed effects, cluster bootstrap by area)

9,558 listings that state bedrooms and bathrooms, 839 areas. Coefficients compare listings within the same area.

| term     |   coef | Effect                              | 95% interval    |
|:---------|-------:|:------------------------------------|:----------------|
| log_size | 0.7922 | +10% size: +7.8% price              | +7.2% to +8.4%  |
| bedrooms | 0.0542 | +1 bedroom at the same size: +5.6%  | +4.1% to +7.2%  |
| baths    | 0.0826 | +1 bathroom at the same size: +8.6% | +6.8% to +10.3% |

Size elasticity 0.79: price rises less than proportionally with size, so larger homes are cheaper per square foot within the same area.
